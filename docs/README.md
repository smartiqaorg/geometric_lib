# Math formulas
## Area
- Circle: S = πR²
- Rectangle: S = ab
- Square: S = a²

## Perimeter
- Circle: P = 2πR
- Rectangle: P = 2a + 2b
- Square: P = 4a

## Symbols and units

- R is the radius of a circle.
- a and b are the side lengths of a rectangle.
- a is the side length of a square.
- Use the same unit for all lengths in a calculation.
- Perimeter is measured in linear units; area is measured in square units.

## Calculation examples

| Shape | Dimensions | Area | Perimeter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Circle | R = 3 cm | 9π ≈ 28.27 cm² | 6π ≈ 18.85 cm |
| Rectangle | a = 3 cm, b = 4 cm | 12 cm² | 14 cm |
| Square | a = 5 cm | 25 cm² | 20 cm |

Circle results are rounded to two decimal places.
